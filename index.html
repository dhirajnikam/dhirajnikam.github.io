Dhiraj Nikam The Whole Stack, 2026 Mobile UI, AI services, data. One maker, every layer. {{ layerNote }}
Artist statement

I treat software the way a printmaker treats a plate: every layer matters, and the last one is the one people touch.

4+ years building production Flutter apps and the AI-powered backends behind them, mostly for teams whose software has to work in the field: solar and wind O&M engineers, site teams, operators. Flutter and Dart on the device; Python (FastAPI, Django, LangChain) and TypeScript (Node.js, React) on the server; AWS Bedrock, PostgreSQL and Neo4j underneath. Open-source author on pub.dev and the VS Code Marketplace.
